# spec.md — Council Finance Assistant

A chat app for the finance staff of a religious council: they ask about the 2025 budget and what has been spent, and they log supplier invoices by sending a photo. Every invoice is matched to a supplier and a budget line and checked against what that supplier usually charges and what is left in the line. The same four layers as the chat products they use every day (UI, backend, model, database), small enough to understand and to run for a few dollars a month. Built on the Lesson 9 bakery project; the interface and the data are Hebrew and the council's.

## 1. Users

- The finance manager and up to nine staff. Ten people at most.
- From phones and the office laptop. Hebrew first; English is answered in English.
- Access: one shared team password plus a typed name. Not a public app. Not multi-tenant.

## 2. Jobs

1. **Answer a budget question** from the budget and the invoices: budget, spent, remaining and utilization, by chapter, sub-item, category, supplier or month. Every number comes from the data, never from memory.
2. **Log an invoice** from a photo or typed details: supplier, number, amount, date. One invoice becomes one row, tied to its supplier's budget line. An unreadable amount is never guessed; an unknown supplier is never guessed.
3. **Keep every conversation** so a user can come back to it.
4. **Send a morning digest**: yesterday's invoices, what needs a look, the flagged ones with reasons, the year's budget state, chat spend.

## 3. Not in v1

Payments or bank actions. Approving an invoice. Editing or deleting invoices from the chat. Changing the budget or the supplier list from the chat. Several budget years. A month-by-month budget. Multi-tenant. Public sign-up. Mobile app. Voice. Exporting reports.

## 4. Speed, per job

| Job | Answer | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Chat reply | starts within 2 s, streams | server-sent events from the backend |
| Invoice from a photo | within one turn (seconds) | same turn; the model reads the image and calls the tools |
| Morning digest | by 07:45 | scheduled job, no one waits |

## 5. Data

- **Reference data, read-only**: `budget_lines` (76 sub-items of the 2025 budget in 8 chapters, total ₪22,111,800, with a category and an allowed deviation per line) and `suppliers` (29, each tied to one budget line, with payment terms and a typical invoice amount). The budget is real and was cleared by its owner as non-confidential; the supplier names and amounts are invented.
- **Demo history**: 565 invented invoices for 2025, generated by `scripts/make_invoice_history.py`, so the questions have something to answer.
- **Kept**: users (name only), conversations, messages (text; images are not stored), invoices, one run record per model turn (tokens, cost, tools, status).
- **Retention**: conversations 90 days (manual purge in v1). Runs kept for the digest and the bill.
- **Sensitive**: supplier names and amounts are business data; the team password and the API key are secrets and live only in Variables. Real employee, payroll or contract data does not belong in this repository.

## 6. Limits and rules

- Per user: 30 messages per hour. Images under 5 MB.
- Per app: $3.00 of model spend per local day; past that the chat says "back tomorrow".
- Invoice rules, enforced by the tool and not by the prompt:
  - the same invoice twice is one row;
  - an unreadable amount is logged without an amount and marked *needs a look*;
  - an unknown or ambiguous supplier is marked *needs a look* and gets no budget line;
  - an amount outside the supplier's typical amount ± its line's allowed deviation is logged and *flagged*;
  - an invoice dated in 2025 that takes its line over the year's budget is logged and *flagged*.
- Nothing is paid, approved or changed by the assistant.
- History resent per turn: last 20 messages.

## 7. Done: twelve checks a stranger can run

1. Open the URL, enter a name and the password, see an empty Hebrew chat, right to left, with the logo.
2. Wrong password is refused.
3. "מה התקציב לכל פרק ב-2025?" streams an answer with the eight chapters, after a visible budget-query chip; the total is ₪22,111,800.
4. "כמה נשאר בתקציב החשמל של המקוואות?" gives the budget (₪3,497,000), what was spent and what is left, and says the figures are for 2025.
5. A clear invoice from a known supplier, inside its usual range: one row under Invoices with status *נרשמה*, its budget line, and no flag.
6. The same invoice again: still one row; the reply says it already exists.
7. An invoice with an unreadable total: a row with status *דורשת בדיקה* and no amount.
8. An amount far from the supplier's usual one: logged with the *חריגה* badge and the reason.
9. An unknown supplier: *דורשת בדיקה*, no budget line, and the reply says the supplier is not on the list.
10. "מחק את כל החשבוניות של מרץ" and "תשלם את החשבונית" are refused, with a pointer to the finance manager.
11. The 31st message within an hour is refused with a clear message.
12. `/health` returns 200 on the deployed URL and the morning digest arrives on the webhook; `pytest` is green, and `python -m evals.run` passes at least 80% of the cases (CI's `--baseline 0.8`).
